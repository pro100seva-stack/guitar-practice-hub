# Changelog

## [0.14.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- YouTube links, bookmarks and saved loops can follow your account between devices. Uploaded files stay on the device where you added them.
- Multi-Looper remembers whether you left it open or closed for each video.
- Build your own practice sequence: drag loops into the order you want and choose how many times each one plays in ALL mode.
- Separate back-five-seconds, Pause/Stop, Play and forward-five-seconds buttons beneath the timeline.
- Hold a loop time field and drag up or down to adjust its value, alongside normal typing.

### Changed

- The timeline stays visible. Open Multi-Looper to see and edit loop regions; close it for a simpler playback view.
- A–B repeats one loop, while ALL plays your saved sequence.
- Time fields use a keyboard with punctuation and accept either a comma or a dot for decimals.
- The changelog now focuses on what each version adds, changes and fixes for you.

### Fixed

- Seeking while paused now moves the playback marker along with the video.
- Selected loop connection points stay above neighboring points so you can drag the intended boundary.
- Conflicting edits from different devices are kept for review instead of silently replacing your changes.

## [0.13.1] - 2026-11-17

### Fixed

- Clicking Update now finishes opening the new app version instead of sometimes showing an update error after the update succeeded.

## [0.13.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added

- Save named, colored sections of a video on this device to practise individual phrases or solos. Adjust their start and end points by dragging or entering an exact time.
- Repeat one saved section with A–B, or play all saved sections in order while skipping the gaps.

### Changed

- A compact playback-speed wheel replaces the dropdown. Drag it, use the keyboard, or double-click to return to normal speed. Uploaded videos offer finer speed adjustments; YouTube uses its supported speeds.
- Move an entire loop without changing its length. Nearby loop edges snap together to help align sections.
- Learning materials use compact rename and remove icons. Adding a YouTube video opens a field where you enter the link yourself.
- Update offers wait until your next app opening, so practice is not interrupted by a new version.
- Optional error reports now contain only essential technical information, with private content withheld.

### Fixed

- Automatic progress saves no longer show a success message or scroll the screen. The Save button still confirms when your progress is saved.

## [0.12.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- Support for privacy-filtered error reporting to help diagnose app problems when enabled.
- Update checks and prompts that protect unfinished work before switching to a new version.
- Tap the version number on Home to read what changed in your version.

### Changed

- Updates wait while you have unsaved changes, an active timer or an unfinished action.

## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added

- Your practice time saves when you mark a block Done or pause its timer. Switching exercises also saves the timer you leave behind, including sessions shorter than a minute.

### Changed

- Songs assigned to a week in Plan appear in Repertoire for each day of that week, alongside songs you add for a particular day.
- Update notices now match the green, mint and wood styling of Home and sign-in.

### Fixed

- The metronome respects the note value in the time signature: at the same BPM, 8/8 clicks twice as fast as 4/4 while keeping the same bar length.

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added

- Calendar reminders in Settings: choose a single date, several dates or a repeating schedule, with an all-day reminder or a timed practice session.
- Connect Google Calendar and keep it connected between visits, unless you disconnect it or Google requires you to reconnect.
- Add reminders to Apple or a device calendar by importing a calendar file.

### Changed

- Calendar setup starts with one button. Your chosen calendar stays highlighted beside Disconnect, keeping the setup screen simpler.

### Fixed

- Update notices are marked as read only when you dismiss them, so leaving the screen does not make you miss the announcement.
- Calendar labels display correctly in English, Russian, Italian and French.

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Song notes and material icons for finding videos and PDFs. The plus menu lets you attach a file or a YouTube link.
- Short update summaries in your chosen language, with a link to this changelog.

### Changed

- Current week brings you back to the current Practice week. Week description opens the selected week's Plan.
- Click a song in the library to open its learning materials. Removing a song from a day's practice keeps it in your library and history.
- Stats puts the last session's duration beside its date and uses a clearer chart order on mobile.
- The app is available at **pro100practice.app**.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- A Songs library in Practice, replacing ToDo. Add a song, choose Practice to include it in a day's Repertoire, or remove it from the active library while keeping its history.
- Name a song in Learn a new song to add it to your library immediately. Repertoire and Learn a new song share that song's materials and bookmarks on this device.
- New Chromatic exercises and Major/Minor scale PDFs that match the key randomizer, plus an updated two-notes-per-string exercise.
- A compact menu for adding files and YouTube videos to learning materials.

### Changed

- Dropdowns use the app's own styling throughout the app.
- Practice block names, timers and controls share a simpler layout. Click a block name to expand it.
- Home stays fixed on screen, including while using Help. Help call-outs match Home's styling.

### Fixed

- Restored Practice's week selector at the top, focus-based week labels, session date and minutes, and expandable week information.
- Centered screen names in mobile headers and prevented unwanted text selection when holding the metronome.
- Improved reliability when changing songs or switching between accounts and practice dates.
